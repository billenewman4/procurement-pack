The project, in four sentences

We're building a free, open-source Claude extension pack for hardware procurement — your two skills (context-aware part search, Gmail order tracking) on top of a shared parts database, modeled on the gbrain/gstack playbook: no app, no UI, paste-one-prompt install, Claude is the interface. The free tool is the wedge; the database is the asset — seeing what small hardware teams actually order is what tells Westgate Supply what to stock and who to sell to. Long-term there's a third asset: the commodity map — which parts actually work for which applications, provenance we're already capturing per line item. Wedge market per the strategy sessions: robotics startups under 10 people, fasteners/off-the-shelf parts, instead of McMaster.

Get started — two things, ~5 minutes

1

Get the connection string from Eshan

He's sending it privately (it's the database password — not in the repo, not in this page). Have it ready to paste.

2

Open Claude Code in your terminal and paste this

Fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/billenewman4/procurement-pack/main/TEAM_SETUP.md and follow it to set me up on the shared BOM database.

Your Claude does the rest: pulls the repo, installs bomdb, asks you for the connection string, registers the server, proves the connection by showing you the live projects, wires up your Claude Desktop config for you, installs the skills, and walks you through the one click only you can do (saving the skills to your account — don't skip it, it's the most-missed step).

3

Fully quit and reopen Claude Desktop, then verify

New local chat (cloud sessions can't see bomdb — it's a local server), and ask:

What projects are in the bomdb database?

You should see the air-quality monitor with its specs and BOM. That's Eshan's live data — same database, both machines.

4

Pick your lane

Join the air monitor — just talk about it: "what's still needed on the air monitor BOM?" Everything you add, Eshan sees instantly, and vice versa.

Or start your own build — paste, with your own project:

I'm starting a new hardware project: [one line on what you're building]. Set it up in my BOM database — create the project, then interview me briefly for my specs (power, connectors, materials, constraints) and store each one. Keep it to one round of questions, then read the specs back to me.

Two honest caveats — also stated in TEAM_SETUP.md: (1) bomdb is a locally-registered server, so cloud sessions (claude.ai, cloud Desktop containers) can't reach it — work in Claude Code or local Desktop chats. (2) No per-user isolation yet, by design: we both see and write everything, and concurrent edits to the same line item are last-write-wins. Fine at two people; it's on the deferral list with a trigger.
